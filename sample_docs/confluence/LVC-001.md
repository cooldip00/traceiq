## Overview

This Lean Value Case supports the initiation of the Customer Loyalty Program initiative.
It provides the business justification required to move from idea to active planning.

## Portfolio Epic Description

Enable a points-based customer loyalty system that rewards repeat purchases,
increases average order value, and reduces customer churn by 15% over 12 months.

## Business Outcome Hypothesis

If we launch a tiered loyalty program (Silver, Gold, Platinum), then repeat purchase
rate will increase by 20% and Net Promoter Score (NPS) will improve by 10 points
within 6 months of launch.

## Leading Indicators

- Weekly active loyalty members after 30 days of launch
- Average points earned per transaction
- Redemption rate of loyalty rewards

## Scope

### In Scope
- Points accrual on all purchase channels (web, mobile, in-store)
- Tiered membership levels: Silver, Gold, Platinum
- Rewards redemption at checkout
- Customer loyalty dashboard (web and mobile)
- Email notifications for tier upgrades and point milestones

### Out of Scope
- Integration with third-party loyalty platforms
- Physical loyalty cards
- Partner brand reward redemption

## Analysis Summary

**Recommendation: GO**

Financial modelling indicates a 14-month payback period with projected incremental
revenue of $4.2M in year one. Risk is rated Medium due to dependency on the new
unified customer profile service.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- System availability: 99.9% uptime during peak retail hours
- Loyalty point calculation latency: < 500ms
- Compliance: PCI-DSS for payment-linked rewards redemption
