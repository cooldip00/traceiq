## Overview

This Product Requirements Document provides the detailed feature specifications
for the Customer Loyalty Program. Development teams use this document as the
primary delivery reference.

## Requirements

### F-01
**Title:** Member Enrolment Flow
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Approved
**Description:** New customers must be able to enrol in the loyalty programme
during checkout or from their account profile page. Enrolment is free and
requires no additional information beyond what is already in the customer profile.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- AC-01: Enrolment available at checkout and in account settings
- AC-02: Enrolment confirmed with on-screen success message
- AC-03: Welcome email sent upon enrolment
**Traces To:** BRD-001/R-01

### F-02
**Title:** Points Accrual and Tier Management
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Approved
**Description:** The system must accrue points on every eligible purchase and
recalculate the customer's membership tier after each accrual event.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- AC-01: Points accrued automatically post-order fulfilment
- AC-02: Tier status updated within 1 hour of points accrual
- AC-03: Tier upgrade triggers push notification and email
**Traces To:** BRD-001/R-02, BRD-001/R-04

### F-03
**Title:** Customer Loyalty Dashboard
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Approved
**Description:** A dedicated loyalty dashboard accessible from the account area
on web and mobile, displaying tier status, points balance, recent transactions,
and available rewards.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- AC-01: Dashboard loads within 2 seconds on a 4G connection
- AC-02: Points history shows last 12 months of transactions
- AC-03: Available rewards clearly displayed with redemption CTA
- AC-04: Offline mode shows cached data with last-updated timestamp
**Traces To:** BRD-001/R-06, BRD-001/R-07

### F-04
**Title:** Gamification and Bonus Rewards
**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Approved
**Description:** The marketing team must be able to configure time-limited bonus
point events (e.g. 3x points on weekends, double points on a customer's birthday)
through an internal admin panel without requiring engineering involvement.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- AC-01: Admin panel allows creation of bonus point rules with start/end dates
- AC-02: Bonus multiplier applied automatically during eligible transactions
- AC-03: Active bonus events displayed on the customer dashboard
**Traces To:** BRD-001/R-04
