## Overview

This Business Requirements Document defines the functional and non-functional
requirements for the Customer Loyalty Program. It serves as the single source of
truth for all downstream design and delivery documents.

## Requirements

### R-01
**Title:** Loyalty Programme Initiation
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Approved
**Description:** The organisation must establish a points-based loyalty programme
that is accessible across all customer-facing channels (web, mobile app, in-store kiosk).
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- AC-01: Programme accessible via web portal within 60 days of go-live
- AC-02: Programme accessible via mobile app within 90 days of go-live
- AC-03: In-store kiosk integration completed within 120 days of go-live
**Traces To:** CDD-001/C-01, PRD-001/F-01

### R-02
**Title:** Tiered Membership Model
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Approved
**Description:** The system must support three membership tiers — Silver, Gold, and
Platinum — with clearly defined point thresholds and benefits per tier.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- AC-01: Silver tier activated at 0 points (all registered customers)
- AC-02: Gold tier activated at 500 cumulative points
- AC-03: Platinum tier activated at 2000 cumulative points
- AC-04: Tier downgrade occurs if thresholds not maintained within a rolling 12-month window
**Traces To:** CDD-001/C-02, PRD-001/F-02

### R-03
**Title:** Registration Email Confirmation Timing
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Approved
**Description:** Upon successful loyalty programme enrolment, the system must send
a confirmation email to the customer within 2 minutes of registration completion.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- AC-01: Confirmation email dispatched within 2 minutes of enrolment event
- AC-02: Email contains programme summary, current tier, and points balance
- AC-03: Email delivery failure triggers a retry within 5 minutes
**Traces To:** PRD-001/F-03

### R-04
**Title:** Points Accrual Rules
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Approved
**Description:** Customers must earn 1 loyalty point for every $1 spent across all
eligible purchase channels. Points must be credited within 24 hours of purchase confirmation.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- AC-01: 1 point per $1 on web and mobile channels
- AC-02: Points posted to account within 24 hours of order fulfilment
- AC-03: Bonus points events (e.g. 3x weekends) configurable by marketing team
**Traces To:** PRD-001/F-02

### R-05
**Title:** Rewards Redemption
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Approved
**Description:** Customers must be able to redeem accumulated points at checkout
for a discount equivalent to $0.01 per point, with a minimum redemption threshold of 100 points.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- AC-01: Redemption available at web and mobile checkout
- AC-02: Minimum redemption threshold: 100 points ($1.00 discount)
- AC-03: Partial redemption allowed; remaining points retained
- AC-04: Redemption transaction is atomic with the purchase transaction
**Traces To:** PRD-001/F-02, CDD-001/C-03

### R-06
**Title:** Customer Loyalty Dashboard
**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Approved
**Description:** Customers must have access to a personal loyalty dashboard showing
their current tier, total points balance, transaction history (last 12 months),
and available rewards.
**Traces To:** PRD-001/F-03

### R-07
**Title:** Mobile Offline Support
**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Approved
**Description:** The mobile application must support offline viewing of the loyalty
dashboard, displaying the last known tier and points balance when no network
connection is available. Point accrual and redemption require connectivity.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- AC-01: Dashboard readable in offline mode using cached data
- AC-02: Offline data refresh timestamp displayed to the customer
- AC-03: Redemption and accrual actions disabled and labelled when offline
**Traces To:** PRD-001/F-03
