## Overview

As a loyalty member, I want to see my points balance update after I complete
a purchase, so that I know my points were recorded correctly.

## Requirements

### US-03
**Title:** Automatic Points Accrual After Purchase
**Priority:** High
**Status:** In Sprint
**Description:** After a successful order is fulfilled, the customer's loyalty
points must be automatically accrued and reflected in their dashboard balance.
No manual action should be required from the customer or support team.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- AC-01: Points posted to loyalty account within 24 hours of order fulfilment
- AC-02: Points transaction visible in dashboard history with order reference
- AC-03: Push notification sent to mobile app on points posting
- AC-04: Email notification sent with points earned and new balance
**Traces To:** PRD-001/F-02, BRD-001/R-04
