## Overview

As a loyalty member, I want to receive a notification when I reach a new
membership tier, so that I know about my upgraded benefits.

## Requirements

### US-04
**Title:** Tier Upgrade Notification
**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Backlog
**Description:** When a customer's points balance crosses a tier threshold
(500 for Gold, 2000 for Platinum), they must receive a congratulatory
notification informing them of their new tier and updated benefits.
**Traces To:** PRD-001/F-02
