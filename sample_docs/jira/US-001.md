## Overview

As a registered customer, I want to enrol in the loyalty programme from my
account settings page, so that I can start earning points on my purchases.

## Requirements

### US-01
**Title:** Customer Self-Enrolment
**Priority:** High
**Status:** In Sprint
**Description:** A registered customer must be able to enrol in the loyalty
programme from their account settings page. Enrolment must be completed
in a single step with no additional form fields required.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- AC-01: "Join Loyalty Programme" button visible on account settings page
- AC-02: Clicking enrol updates customer status to loyalty_member in the database
- AC-03: Success banner displayed on screen immediately after enrolment
- AC-04: Confirmation email sent within 2 minutes of enrolment
**Traces To:** PRD-001/F-01
