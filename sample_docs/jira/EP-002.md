## Overview

This Epic covers the backend implementation of the points accrual engine,
tier calculation logic, and all related customer notifications.

## Epic Objective

Automate the points accrual lifecycle so that every eligible purchase results
in accurate points posting and tier recalculation without manual intervention.

## Requirements

### EP-02
**Title:** Points Accrual Engine Epic Scope
**Priority:** High
**Status:** In Progress
**Description:** Implement the Kafka consumer for order events, the points
calculation logic, the tier engine, and the notification triggers for tier
upgrades and point milestones.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- AC-01: Points accrued on 100% of completed orders within 24 hours
- AC-02: Tier recalculation triggered on every accrual event
- AC-03: Zero duplicate point postings in production (idempotency enforced)
**Traces To:** PRD-001/F-02
