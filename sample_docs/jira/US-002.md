## Overview

As a loyalty member, I want to see my current points balance and membership tier
on my loyalty dashboard, so that I know how close I am to the next tier.

## Requirements

### US-02
**Title:** Points Balance and Tier Display
**Priority:** High
**Status:** In Sprint
**Description:** The loyalty dashboard must display the customer's current points
balance, their active membership tier (Silver/Gold/Platinum), points required
for the next tier, and a progress indicator.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- AC-01: Dashboard displays current points balance prominently
- AC-02: Active tier badge (Silver / Gold / Platinum) displayed
- AC-03: Progress bar showing points to next tier
- AC-04: Confirmation email sent to customer within 60 minutes of loyalty enrolment
**Traces To:** PRD-001/F-03, BRD-001/R-03

## SEEDED CONFLICT:
## AC-04 states email within 60 minutes.
## BRD-001/R-03 requires email within 2 minutes.
